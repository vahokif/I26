# Deviations

Intentional differences from the original. Rendering choices never feed back
into the simulation.

**Rendering is modern by default** — better than the original is the goal,
not a faithful copy. A **1997 mode** (Graphic Detail's 1997 Mode, kept with the settings and applied on resuming a mission; F12 in game flips and saves it; `--1997` forces it on for one run, leaving the saved setting; the shell takes a change from the next start or mission) reproduces the
original look where that is known: the engine's terrain light model, no
shadows or post-processing, flat sky. Gameplay and physics are faithful in
both modes.
